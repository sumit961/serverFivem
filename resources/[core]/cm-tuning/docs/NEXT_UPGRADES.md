# Recommended next additions

1. **Mechanic job mode**
   - Public self-service shops and employee-operated workshops.
   - Labour percentage, society revenue, invoices and employee permissions.

2. **Vehicle class restrictions**
   - Different upgrade availability and pricing for motorcycles, electric cars, emergency vehicles and heavy vehicles.

3. **Dyno system**
   - Before/after horsepower, torque, acceleration and top-speed runs.
   - Save dyno history per vehicle.

4. **Install animations and workshop props**
   - Bonnet opening, mechanic animation, lift position, tools and timed installation by category.

5. **Part inventory and stock**
   - Engines, turbos, tyres and cosmetic parts supplied through the CM inventory/economy.
   - Workshop stock, supplier orders and limited parts.

6. **Receipts and logs**
   - CM Admin logs for quote, payment, refund, installed parts and rejected exploit attempts.
   - Vehicle service history visible in the vehicle information UI.

7. **Preset builds**
   - Save named cosmetic/performance presets.
   - Preview a full build and purchase it as one server-priced transaction.

8. **Insurance and legality**
   - Street-legal vs illegal upgrades, police inspection, defect notices and insurance impact.

9. **Custom RGB paint**
   - Server-controlled RGB/pearlescent paint with pricing and correct persistence.

10. **Sound and feedback**
    - Engine-rev preview, turbo spool preview, horn preview and subtle UI sounds.
