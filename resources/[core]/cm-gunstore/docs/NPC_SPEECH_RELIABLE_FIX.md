NPC SPEECH RELIABLE FIX
=======================

Fixes random NPC dialog not appearing reliably.

Changes:
- NPC now speaks immediately when player enters speech distance.
- NPC repeats a random greeting only on cooldown while player stays nearby.
- NPC draws a clearer 3D speech bubble above the head.
- NPC also plays a GTA native ambient voice bark at the same time.
- Farewell text + voice plays after selecting "No thanks".

Note:
FiveM/GTA cannot voice custom written sentences directly. The written custom sentence is drawn as text above the NPC, while the ped plays a matching native voice bark such as GENERIC_HI or GENERIC_BYE.
