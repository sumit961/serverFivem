# cm-ems v4.0.0

- Multi-responder dispatch incidents with priority, patient count, status, backup, decline, GPS and persistent history.
- Nearby calls can join one government doctor response; the doctor treats every queued downed player before leaving.
- Configurable two-minute automatic AI response, shared-response radius, billing and medic rewards.
- Expanded EMS ranks and server-enforced hospital, fleet, reporting and management permissions.
- Resource-owned cm-admin management page for hiring, firing, suspension, settings and leadership.
- Permanent medical reports and searchable patient history.
