# cm-family v1.9.7

## Explicit family vehicle registration

- Replaced the player-facing shared/private vehicle control with explicit family-garage registration.
- Family managers with `family.manage_vehicles` can register eligible member vehicles and set the minimum family rank.
- Family vehicle management lists expose personal vehicle candidates only to authorized managers; ordinary members see registered family vehicles only.
- Legacy share actions fail closed while existing registration records continue to authorize family vehicle access.
