# cm-family v1.6.1 — Meeting points

- Adds the `family.set_meeting` rank permission.
- Authorized members can broadcast their current position from the family dashboard.
- Every online family member receives the GPS waypoint, notification, and waypoint alert sound.
- Server validates membership, permission, coordinates, and proximity to the sender's server-side ped.
- Adds a ten-second server cooldown and durable meeting-point audit entry.
